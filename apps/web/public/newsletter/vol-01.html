From the Desk of the Chairman

For nearly four decades, Thamserku has been built on something more enduring than ambition — a deep respect for the mountains, the communities that live beside them, and the climbers who return to them, again and again.

Today, that same spirit takes a new form. Thamserku Expedition now stands as a dedicated Himalayan expedition house — Sherpa-led, operationally precise, and guided by a philosophy that treats every journey as something worth preparing for with care.

The work is the same as it has always been: careful preparation, a small circle of trusted hands on the rope, and an understanding that the mountain decides what is possible. What changes is the architecture around it — clearer, calmer, and shaped to fit the climber as much as the objective.

“The mountain is no longer merely a summit objective. It is a personal journey — a test of discipline, of humility, and of what we are capable of when we are truly prepared.”

02 — The Expedition Architecture

Five Editions. One Philosophy.

Our Expedition Architecture is a structured approach to designing Himalayan expeditions that honour both the mountain and the climber. Each Edition reflects a distinct philosophy and style — not a price point. All expeditions are fully customisable.
A

Alpine

Pure alpinism. Minimal structure, fast decision-making, and essential support only. For committed climbers who value independence, technical purity, and lightness.
B

Bespoke

Fully personalised and modular. Co-designed with our guiding team around your goals, timeline, and preferences. A climber's journey built around them, not a template.
C

Crafted

A balanced premium expedition: strong performance focus, elevated comfort, and curated logistics. Precision and refinement at every stage of the ascent.
D

Definitive

Our fully supported flagship experience. Comprehensive logistics, world-class Sherpa leadership, and elevated hospitality from day one. Nothing left unconsidered.
E

Explorer

Transformational journeys beyond conventional itineraries. Exclusive routes, rare objectives, and deeply immersive experiences for explorers and elite climbers.

Autumn in the Himalaya

Every autumn offers a brief window when the Himalayas reveal their finest climbing conditions. From celebrated classics to remote exploratory objectives, our 2026 programme combines proven expedition expertise with authentic exploration.

ROUTE North-East Ridge, Nepal

ALT  7,132 m ROUTE  North-West, Nepal EDITION  E · Explorer

For climbers seeking something beyond the familiar, Api Himal offers one of the most compelling objectives in Nepal today. Its remarkable isolation, pristine glaciers and untouched alpine terrain evoke the spirit of Himalayan exploration that has largely disappeared from more commercial peaks.

Rising as the highest peak of Nepal's remote Api–Nampa massif, it is one of the country's least-climbed 7,000ers. First ascended by a Japanese expedition in 1960, it has seen only a handful of successful summits, the last recorded in 2015. For Autumn 2026, Thamserku returns following an extensive reconnaissance to assess access, glacier conditions and the climbing route.

“Where exploration returns to the Himalayas.”

A Blessing for the Season

Miyolangsangma

In Sherpa tradition, the golden goddess who rides a tiger is the guardian deity of Chomolungma — Everest. One of the Five Long-Life Sisters, she is a giver of food, fortune and safe passage, and climbers have long sought her blessing before setting out. We open this new chapter under her gaze.
05 — Chairman's Message

On stewardship, new paths, and the journey ahead.

Dear Adventurers,

Thamserku has always been more than a company. Since 1987, it has been a form of stewardship — of the mountains, the craft of high-altitude guiding, and of the trust placed in us by climbers from every corner of the world.

Having completed the Seven Summits in 2023, I returned with a clearer conviction that the most meaningful journeys are not those defined by the peaks reached, but by the preparation, the relationships, and the understanding earned along the way.

With Thamserku Expedition, we have built something that reflects that belief. Our new Expedition Architecture — from Alpine to Explorer — is not a product catalogue. It is a framework for matching each climber's ambition and identity to the right kind of expedition. We remain proudly Nepalese, deeply mountain-rooted, and fully committed to safety, precision and hospitality at every stage.

Sonam Sherpa
Chairman & Founder — Thamserku Expedition
