At 7,132 m, Api Himal stands as the highest summit of Nepal's remote Api–Nampa massif — first summited by a Japanese team in 1960, and visited by only a handful of expeditions since. The last successful ascent was recorded in 2015. For most of the past decade, Api Himal has simply been left alone.

This October, Thamserku returns — following an extensive reconnaissance to assess access, glacier conditions, and route viability — to offer climbers something increasingly rare in the Himalaya: genuine first-generation exploration.

“Some mountains are climbed. Others are discovered.”
02 — A Rare First-Ascent Objective

Two 7,000ers. One Expedition.

Beyond the summit of Api Himal lies an extraordinary opportunity. Api West (7,100 m) remains unclimbed, presenting ambitious mountaineers with the chance to participate in a potential first ascent.

This expedition combines the ascent of an established Himalayan giant with one of the most compelling exploratory objectives available anywhere in the Greater Himalaya today. It is designed not simply to reach a summit — but to contribute to Himalayan mountaineering history.
03 — Why Thamserku

Built on decades of Himalayan judgment.

For nearly four decades, Thamserku has been at the forefront of Himalayan exploration. Built upon generations of Sherpa knowledge, world-class logistics, and a legacy of pioneering expeditions, we continue to seek mountains that inspire genuine exploration rather than crowded success.

From professional planning and expedition infrastructure to IFMGA/UIAGM leadership and a dedicated team of climbing Sherpas, every detail has been designed to support safe, efficient, and meaningful exploration in one of Nepal's most remote mountain regions.

It is the kind of preparation that only comes from having done this — repeatedly, and at altitude — for a long time.

“Where exploration returns to the Himalayas.”

For climbers who believe the greatest adventures still lie beyond the familiar, this is more than an expedition — it is an opportunity to become part of the continuing story of Himalayan exploration.
04 — Reserve Your Place

Eight climbers. One departure.

Given the exclusive nature of this exploratory expedition, Thamserku accepts a maximum of eight climbers. Early inquiry is strongly recommended.
